About Me

The person behind the cuts

I'm a Multan-based video editor working remotely with clients across social and digital platforms. My day-to-day lives inside CapCut Pro, with After Effects for motion graphics and a growing AI-assisted workflow — ElevenLabs for voice, and tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to speed up scripting and ideation. I edit both ends of the spectrum: quick, punchy short-form cuts for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok, and slower, structured long-form and documentary-style pieces.

Color correction, subtitle sync, and sound design aren't afterthoughts for me — they're where a video goes from "finished" to "watchable." I'm currently completing my Bachelor's degree at Bahauddin Zakariya University while building this freelance practice, which has sharpened how I manage time, deadlines, and communication with remote clients. What sets me apart is pairing traditional editing discipline with an AI-forward toolkit, so I can move quickly without cutting corners on craft.
